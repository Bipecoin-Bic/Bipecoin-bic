1. Abstract
 
Bipecoin (BIC) is a community-driven BEP20 token on BNB Smart Chain with a fixed supply of 5,000,000,000 BIC. The contract is verified on BscScan and published on GitHub. No further minting is possible. Bipecoin follows DAOR — Decentralized, Autonomous, Open & Renounced.

3. Burn Schedule
 
400M Bipecoin burned every 5 years.
The burn is done by sending tokens to the dead burn address, permanently removing them from circulation. This deflationary mechanism reduces supply over time and increases scarcity.
 
4. Lock Schedule
 
3.5B of Bipecoin locked in PinkLock from September 2026 to September 2027.
This includes 1,000,000,000 BIC and 2,500,000,000 BIC (PinkLock Record 1894705) locked in PinkLock platform. The lock period ensures team commitment and prevents early selling. All locks are verifiable on PinkLock platform. Ownership is renounced, owner balance 0.00%.
